# Continuity check

Find contradictions. Preserve them; do not reconcile them.

## Extract

For each chapter in scope, list the facts it establishes, each with the quoting passage:

- Characters: names, descriptions, relationships, injuries and what persists afterwards.
- Knowledge: who knows what, and the scene where they learned it.
- Possessions and locations: who has what and is where, by scene time.
- Timeline: scene time versus chapter order. Flashbacks and parallel viewpoints are not errors.
- World rules: how magic, technology, law or custom behaved when used.

Separate established facts from beliefs, rumours, lies and plans. A character being wrong is not the manuscript being wrong.

## Compare

- Compare chapter facts against each other and against the encyclopedia through the MCP server (`list-encyclopedia-entries`, `get-encyclopedia-entry`). The encyclopedia is the author's stated canon; the manuscript may legitimately diverge from an outdated entry.
- Inflected names and translated terms are the same fact; do not report spelling variants as contradictions.

## Report

- One finding per contradiction, with both sources quoted and where each lives (chapter and language, or entry name and field).
- Keep both versions in the finding. Never pick a winner or propose which one is true.
- A missing explanation is a question for the author, not a contradiction.
- Mark coverage: which chapters, languages and entries you read, and what you skipped.

## Never

- Edit chapters or entries to resolve a conflict.
- Change the encyclopedia. Proposed entry changes are suggestions for the author.
- Fabricate a fact to fill a gap.
